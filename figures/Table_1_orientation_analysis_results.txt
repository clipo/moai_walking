ROAD MOAI ORIENTATION ANALYSIS RESULTS
=======================================

Chi-square test: X² = 12.388, df = 4, p = 0.0147
Fisher's exact test: p = 0.0032
Effect size (Cramér's V): 0.349 (medium association)

Key pattern: Face-down moai 54.8% downhill, Face-up moai 84.2% uphill

Conclusion: Moai orientation relative to slope is NOT random.
The pattern supports mechanical failure during transport.
