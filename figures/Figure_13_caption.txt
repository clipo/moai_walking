Figure 13. Analysis of moai size in relation to transport distance. (A) Box plots showing size distributions across transport phases, with median values showing slight decrease from early to late phases. (B) Scatter plot with regression line demonstrating a weak negative correlation between statue size and distance from the quarry (r = -0.216, p = 0.149, n = 46). The shaded area represents 95% confidence intervals for the regression. The lack of a strong correlation between size and transport distance supports the walking hypothesis, suggesting the transport technique was sophisticated enough to move statues of various sizes effectively. The presence of large moai at all distances indicates that statue size was not the primary limiting factor for transport distance, consistent with a versatile and robust walking technique that could accommodate the full range of moai dimensions.
